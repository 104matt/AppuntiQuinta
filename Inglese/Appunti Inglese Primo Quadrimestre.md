## **L'Impero Britannico: l'Impero "su cui non tramonta mai il sole"**

L'espressione: "The sun never sets on the British Empire" (Sul Regno Unito non tramonta mai il sole).

Significato: L'impero era esteso su così tanti territori in diversi fusi orari in tutto il pianeta che c'era sempre almeno una colonia illuminata dal sole.

**La Gran Bretagna e l'India (L'espansionismo)**

1876: La regina Vittoria viene proclamata Imperatrice d'India (Empress of India).

"The Jewel in the Crown": L'India era definita "il gioiello della corona" per via della sua enorme importanza strategica ed economica per l'Impero. Materia prime e risorse: L'India forniva grandi quantità di risorse naturali essenziali per l'industria britannica, in particolare:

* Cotone (cotton)
* Carbone (coal)
* Ferro e Acciaio (iron and steel)

**La Grande Carestia Irlandese (The Great Famine)**

1845 / 1852: Un parassita delle patate (la peronospora, in inglese late blight) distrusse quasi interamente i raccolti di patate in Irlanda. **Conseguenze:** Gran parte della popolazione soffrì la fame e molte persone morirono per denutrizione e malattie (starvation). il resto della popolazione emigrò verso il canada **Cause:** ostacolo per l’Irlanda e la Corona. From this Ireland started asking for a Home Rule which is independence from the Great Britain.

## **Enclosure Acts and Industrial Revolution**

Before Enclosure Acts there were many pieces of land used freely by farmers. At a certain point the government decided to privatize these pieces of land and this contributed to industrialization as the farmer left their lands to look for work in the industries near the cities.

The **industrial revolution** brought many changes:

Energy:

* Use of Coal
* Use of Steam Power and Steam Engine (Implemented and imrpoved by Watt in the 1800's, it's original creator was Newcomen 1712)

Industry:

* Rapid growth of coal mining iron and steel production.

Transport:

* Steam powered railways
* Steam engine ships made moving move faster

**Social Changes of the Industrial Revolution:**

* Creation of a **modern working class**
* Involvment of **children** within the working enviroment as they were cheaper
  * Involvement of pregnant women as well
  * Work shifts were made of Long Shifts very tiring
  * The living conditions were terrible, sometimes they lived in overcrowded cellars below the street level where sewers happened to break and flood the place
  * This period of time was called the **slums**
* **Luddism** grows where this movement breaks machines as a rebellion
